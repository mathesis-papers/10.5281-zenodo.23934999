# Why Three Variables Are Necessary: An Antipodal Obstruction for Chiral Events

This bilingual book explains the minimality question posed in §2.9 of Lester, Haddadin, and Gripaios: why events with two interchangeable pairs require at least three continuous Lorentz-invariant parity-odd readings. The Lean declaration matches the common-zero lower bound; the exact minimum also uses the sufficient triples in §5.1 of the original paper. The complete technical argument is in [Exact statements and complete proofs](02-statements.md).
